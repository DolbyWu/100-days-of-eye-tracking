# Day 012 · Research through Design reflection

## What inspired it

这件作品来自我听到的一位神经科学家从感官与神经信号角度谈自闭症谱系的分享。分享的原始出处待补。下面的资料是我为这个作品另外核对的研究背景。

**感觉换能（sensory transduction）**是感受器把光、声波带来的机械振动、压力或化学分子等刺激，转化为受体的电活动变化。接下来，神经系统通过电活动和细胞之间的化学信号传递、调节信息。[OpenStax, Sensory Perception](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/14-1-sensory-perception)

在自闭症研究里，谷氨酸和 GABA 常用于讨论兴奋性和抑制性信号；多巴胺、血清素和去甲肾上腺素也是研究过的调节系统；催产素则是一种神经肽。研究问题包括调节方式的差异是否与感知、学习、情绪、注意力或社会互动有关。不同研究测量的是不同层面，也涉及不同人群。例如，一项实验研究了 GABA 与视觉知觉任务之间的关系；儿童探索性研究测的是血浆和尿液里的指标，不能直接外推到脑内突触；一项较大的催产素随机试验没有发现社会功能优于安慰剂。现有线索不能归结为某一种递质，也不能用来概括每个自闭症儿童。[Robertson 等，2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26711497/) · [儿童外周指标探索性研究](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41423608/) · [Sikich 等，2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34644471/)

## How it became an interaction

我想做的是一次关于「信号如何改变一张图」的体验。我把眼动仪采集的注视、眨眼、瞳孔大小和眼睑开合，分别映射到局部亮青、整片闪白、点云呼吸和恢复。三连眨让构图散开，闭眼三秒让它重组。每种变化都配有合成声音，让观众除了看见变化，也能听到它发生。

眼动到神经递质的关联，在这个作品里是灵感与设计映射。作品不从眼动推断脑内化学活动，也不把这些效果对应到特定递质或自闭症特征。下一次观察时，我想问：同样的画面，如果更换反馈的强度、速度和声音，参与者会怎样描述自己的感官体验？

## Reflection prompts

> What if looking did not simply select or control an object, but introduced forces into a world that continued to move beyond our gaze?

## Setup and evidence

- Date, researcher, installation context and viewing distance
- Neon Companion and TouchDesigner versions, machine, preset, grid and output resolution
- Inputs used and intended mappings
- Paths to consented traces, stills, recordings and field notes
- Measured frame rate and latency observations
- Calibration, missing data and interruptions

## Encounter

- When did your gaze feel like a force? Describe a specific moment and its timestamp.
- What moved after you looked away? Compare that moment with direct cursor-like tracking.
- Did holding a fixation feel like gathering energy or waiting for a command?
- Was a blink read as interruption, rhythm or agency? Who noticed the shockwave?
- How did wearables, positional drift, and the gap between scene and display coordinates shape the experience?

## Design iteration

- Record one surprising behavior and distinguish observation from interpretation.
- Compare two presets using the same replay. Which differences came from the mapping and which from the solver?
- Document one failed assumption and a change that responds to it.
- Identify a design tension or provisional knowledge claim. State whose experience it represents and where it might not apply.

## DIS contribution

- Research artifact and materials available for inspection
- Concept or interaction quality this making process exposes
- Evidence, counterexample and limitations
- Next iteration and the question it will let you investigate
